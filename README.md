# Human Resource Analytics Dashboard

### Project Title
**Human Resource Analytics – Employee Attrition and Retention Analysis**

### Short Description / Purpose
I created this Power BI project to analyze employee data and understand the major factors behind employee attrition. The main purpose of this dashboard is to help HR teams understand employee satisfaction, workload, performance, salary levels, and retention patterns. It also helps identify areas where the company can improve its employee retention strategies.

### Tech Stack
- Power BI – Dashboard creation and data visualization
- Power Query – Data cleaning and transformation
- DAX – Creating measures and calculated columns
- Excel / CSV – Data handling

### Data Source
- Kaggle – HR Analytics Dataset
- Dataset Link: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/giripujar/hr-analytics

### Features / Highlights

**1. Business Problem**
- The company is facing employee attrition across different departments.
- HR needs to understand why employees are leaving the organization.
- Factors such as job satisfaction, workload, salary, performance, and promotion opportunities may influence employee retention.
- The company needs a data-driven approach to improve employee satisfaction and reduce attrition.

**2. Goal of the Dashboard**
- Analyze overall employee attrition and retention.
- Identify departments with higher employee attrition.
- Understand the relationship between employee satisfaction and attrition.
- Analyze workload, project allocation, performance, and salary patterns.
- Evaluate employee tenure and promotion opportunities.
- Provide useful insights to support better HR decisions.

**3. Key Visuals**

I divided the dashboard into four interactive pages:

- **Page 1 – HR Executive Overview:** KPI cards, total employees, active employees, attrition count, attrition rate, department-wise employee distribution, and salary-wise attrition analysis.
- **Page 2 – Employee Satisfaction & Attrition:** Satisfaction group analysis, average satisfaction score, performance evaluation, and department-wise attrition rate.
- **Page 3 – Workload, Performance & Salary:** Monthly working hours, number of projects, salary categories, performance evaluation, and their relationship with employee attrition.
- **Page 4 – Employee Retention & HR Strategy:** Retention rate, employee tenure, promotion status, work accidents, and department-wise retention analysis.

**4. Business Impact & Insights**
- Helps HR identify departments that need more attention.
- Makes it easier to understand employee satisfaction and workload patterns.
- Highlights areas related to salary, performance, and career growth that deserve further investigation.
- Supports better workforce planning and employee engagement strategies.
- Helps HR make informed decisions to improve employee retention and reduce employee turnover.

### Project Outcome
Through this project, I learned how to clean and transform HR data, create DAX measures, design interactive dashboards, and convert raw data into meaningful business insights. This project helped me understand how data analytics can support real-world HR decision-making.
